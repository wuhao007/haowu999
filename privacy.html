Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

At Alpha Hub Pro, your privacy is our absolute priority. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in our application.

1. Zero Server-Side Holding Data

We believe in absolute sovereignty. Alpha Hub Pro is designed to run entirely client-side. We do not maintain any servers that store your financial assets, holding units, or portfolio valuations. All calculations, input fields, and portfolio information are processed and saved locally in your browser's local storage (LocalStorage).

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • Local Input: Any financial values or holdings data you enter in the "主权" (Vault) tab is stored only on your local device.
  • License Validation: When you enter a license key, the key is securely sent to Gumroad's official validation endpoints to verify status. We do not store this request on any proprietary server.
  • Google AdSense: We display advertisements using Google AdSense. AdSense may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website. You can learn more or opt out by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following trusted third-party providers for basic features:

  • GitHub Pages: For website hosting.
  • Gumroad: For payment processing and subscription license key verification.
  • Yahoo Finance: Used by our background data pipelines to fetch market prices (no user identification data is shared).

4. Data Security

Since your portfolio data is stored locally in your browser's storage, the security of that data depends on the security of your device and browser. We recommend keeping your system updated and using browser security best practices.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
